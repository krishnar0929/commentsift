CommentSift

Search, sort and export YouTube comments in a clean table. Find what people are saying in seconds instead of scrolling for minutes.

Add to Chrome, it's free Runs entirely in your browser. No account, no tracking.

What you can do

Search every comment

Find any word in comments, usernames or replies. Every match is highlighted.

Sort your way

By likes, number of replies, username or date, which YouTube itself can't do.

Quick filters

Pinned, hearted by the creator, creator replied, or comments with replies.

Replies in place

Open any thread inside the table. Creator replies are clearly marked.

Export to CSV

Save comments as a spreadsheet for Excel or Google Sheets.

Make it yours

Eight panel colours, and it follows YouTube's dark theme.

See it in action

How to use

  1. Install CommentSift from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open any YouTube video and scroll to the comments.
  3. Click Table view next to YouTube's "Sort by", or press Alt+Shift+C.
