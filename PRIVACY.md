# CommentSift — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

CommentSift does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- **What it reads:** only the comments already displayed on the YouTube page you are viewing, in order to show them in a searchable table.
- **Where it runs:** entirely in your browser. The extension has no servers and sends no data anywhere. The only thing it loads is commenters' profile pictures, from YouTube's own image servers, exactly as YouTube shows them on the page.
- **What it saves:** only your interface preferences (panel position and size, panel colour, rows per page, search scope, loading options), stored locally with `chrome.storage.local` on your device.
- **CSV export:** files are created locally and saved only when you click "Export CSV". They are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Permissions:** `storage` (to remember your preferences) and access to `www.youtube.com` (to read comments on the page you are viewing).

CommentSift is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
